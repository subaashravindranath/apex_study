# Feature Roadmap

This roadmap captures a practical, phased plan to ship the tutor experience centered on cars first, then expand to other interests.

## Phase 0 — Discovery & Definition (Week 0–1)
- Define target learner profiles (O-level/A-level, homeschool, self-study).
- Gather 5–10 sample textbooks, notes, and past papers for validation.
- Confirm the “cars first” interest framing and terminology.
- Set measurable success metrics (e.g., weekly active learners, quiz accuracy lift, retention).

## Phase 1 — MVP: Cars-First Tutor (Weeks 2–6)
**Goal:** Deliver the core learning loop with cars as the sole interest domain.

**Learning & Content**
- PDF/scan upload for textbooks, notes, and past papers.
- Content extraction, chunking, and topic mapping.
- RAG-based personalization layer to connect study content with the learner's history and interests.
- Study session generation with car-themed explanations/examples.
- Basic quizzes with auto-grading and feedback.

**Progression & Motivation**
- XP and leveling system tied to mastery scores.
- Starter garage (e.g., Civic) and unlockable cars.
- Simple report card showing strengths, gaps, and next steps.

**Operational**
- Admin tooling for content review and prompt tuning.
- Feedback collection for accuracy and clarity.

## Phase 2 — Personalization & Depth (Weeks 7–12)
**Goal:** Improve learning quality and individual adaptation.

- Adaptive study plan that updates after each session.
- Personalized remediation based on mistakes and confidence.
- Spaced repetition for key concepts.
- Exam-mode practice using past paper formats.
- Improved explanations with difficulty tuning.

## Phase 3 — Social & Engagement (Weeks 13–18)
**Goal:** Increase stickiness and accountability.

- Weekly progress goals and streak alternatives (mastery-based).
- Shareable progress summaries for parents/mentors.
- Study groups and leaderboards by topic mastery.

## Phase 4 — Multi-Interest Expansion (Weeks 19–26)
**Goal:** Expand beyond cars while keeping the interest-to-learning link.

- Add new interest worlds (tech, business, sports, music).
- Interest selection onboarding and auto-migration of progress.
- Unified garage/collection system across interest worlds.

## Phase 5 — Scale & Partnerships (Weeks 27+)
**Goal:** Broaden distribution and trust.

- School and tutoring partner dashboards.
- Curriculum-aligned certification or badges.
- Integrations for LMS export/import.

---

## Risks & Open Questions
- Accuracy of content extraction from diverse textbook formats.
- Ensuring car-themed explanations remain academically rigorous.
- Avoiding over-gamification that distracts from learning.

## Success Metrics
- Weekly active learners (WAL) and retention.
- Improvement in quiz accuracy over time.
- Learner-reported confidence gains.
- Upload-to-first-session conversion rate.
